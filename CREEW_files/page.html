𝐇𝐚𝐧𝐝𝐬-𝐨𝐧 𝐓𝐫𝐚𝐢𝐧𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐨𝐧 "𝐌𝐞𝐚𝐬𝐮𝐫𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐔𝐫𝐛𝐚𝐧 𝐖𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐫 𝐒𝐞𝐜𝐮𝐫𝐢𝐭𝐲" 𝐔𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐖𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐫 𝐒𝐞𝐜𝐮𝐫𝐢𝐭𝐲 𝐀𝐬𝐬𝐞𝐬𝐬𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐓𝐨𝐨𝐥 (𝐖𝐀𝐓𝐒𝐀𝐓)
The ABCD-Centre - Global Water and Climate Adaptation Centre, in collaboration with the Center of Research for Environment, Energy and Water - CREEW, the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), and the IOE, Pulchowk Campus – Tribhuvan University, successfully organized a half-day training workshop on "How to Measure Urban Water Security?" today, 4 September 2026, at the Oxfam ...Office, Lalitpur.
The training brought together 14 participants from government agencies (Federal and Local), Oxfam, ICIMOD, and Practitioner to build a holistic understanding of water security and hands-on skills in using WATSAT 2.0 for objective, indicator-based assessment of a city's water security situation.
We extend our sincere gratitude to our resource persons:
◆ Prof. Mukand Babel – Professor, Water Engineering and Management (WEM) & Director, Centre for Water and Climate Adaptation (CWCA), AIT
◆ Prof. Vishnu Prasad Pandey – Professor, Department of Civil Engineering, Pulchowk Campus, IoE & Executive Director, Centre for International Relations, Tribhuvan University
◆ Dr. Anil Aryal – Executive Director, Center of Research for Environment Energy and Water

Thank you to all participants for their active engagement in strengthening capacities for urban water security assessment in Nepal! We look forward to seeing WATSAT integrated further into mainstream policy and management practices.

🌐 Learn more about WATSAT: http://watsat.org
